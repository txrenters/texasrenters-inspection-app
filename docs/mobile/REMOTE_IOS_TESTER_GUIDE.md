# TexasRenters Beta — iPhone Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test the inspection app. This is a **temporary beta**. You'll use a free
app called **Expo Go** to open it — the real App Store version comes later.

Please read the two notes at the bottom before you start.

## 1. Install Expo Go

Open the App Store on your iPhone, search **Expo Go**, install it. It's free.

## 2. Open the TexasRenters app

Your coordinator will send you a **QR code** (or a link) at the start of each session. The
coordinator must start the **V2** beta before sending it.

- Open the iPhone **Camera** app and point it at the QR code, then tap the banner.
- Or open **Expo Go** and scan it from there.

The app takes a few seconds to load the first time.

## 3. Allow permissions

You'll be asked once for each. Tap **Allow** — the app cannot do an inspection without them.

- **Camera** — recording inspection videos
- **Microphone** — recording your narration
- **Motion** — the guided-recording rotation helper

If you tapped "Don't Allow" by mistake: iPhone **Settings → Expo Go**, and turn them on.

## 4. Sign in

Use the **beta account** your coordinator gave you. Don't use a personal or live account.

## 5. Do a test inspection

1. Open the inspection assigned to you
2. Pick the area you were asked to record
3. Follow the on-screen guide to record the room
4. Take the snapshots it asks for — a **wide** shot first, then a **close-up**
5. Add any findings you notice
6. Wait for uploads to finish

## 6. Confirm before you finish

Check that **pending uploads shows 0** before closing the app. If it doesn't, stay on Wi-Fi or
good cellular signal for a minute — it keeps uploading in the background.

## 7. Reporting a problem

Send your coordinator:

- What you were doing when it happened
- A **screenshot** (press Side + Volume Up)
- The **Copy diagnostics** button on the Settings screen, pasted into your message

Diagnostics are safe to send — they contain no passwords.

## Two important notes

**Push notifications don't work in this beta.** That's expected, not a bug. Expo Go can't
receive them. You'll still see reminders while the app is open. This is fixed in the real App
Store version.

**The app only works while your coordinator's computer is online.** This beta runs from their
machine. If you get connection errors, they may have stopped the session — message them.
Anything you already recorded is saved on your phone and uploads when the connection is back,
so **don't delete anything**.

## Please don't

- Use this beta for a **real tenant inspection** — it's for testing only
- Share the QR code or link outside the test group
- Share your beta account details

The link works only while the coordinator's Docker and V2 Metro session is running.
